# Stellar Threads: Decentralized Fashion Finance & Provenance Architecture
*A Whitepaper on High-Throughput Trade Escrows, Production Factoring, and Digital Product Passports on Stellar Soroban Protocol 22*

## Abstract
The global luxury fashion supply chain suffers from severe liquidity crunches, opaque subcontracting, and rampant counterfeiting. Stellar Threads introduces a high-performance decentralized protocol built on Stellar Soroban providing:
1. **Milestone Trade Escrow**: Programmable 4-stage disbursement tied to verifiable quality audits.
2. **Production Invoice Factoring**: Liquidity pools funding atelier raw material purchases with senior tranche investor yield.
3. **Digital Product Passports (DPP)**: European Union DPP-compliant garment provenance with NFC cryptographic verification.

# ESG Sustainability & Laboratory Authentication Standard

## Required Attestation Credentials
1. **GOTS Certification (Global Organic Textile Standard)**: Minimum 95% certified organic fibers.
2. **Water Conservation Benchmark**: At least 70% reduction in water usage vs conventional cotton.
3. **Zero-Microplastic Certification**: 100% biodegradable natural fibers (bio-silk, organic hemp, linen).

# Production Factoring: Investor & Liquidity Guide

## How Factoring Works
Ateliers often have locked orders from major department stores but lack upfront working capital for raw organic silk and weaving mills.

Investors supply liquidity on Stellar Threads:
1. **Explore Campaigns**: Review atelier reputation scores, order size, and promised APY.
2. **Deploy Capital**: Commit XLM to `production_financing` pool.
3. **Earn Returns**: Upon buyer settlement, investors automatically receive principal plus interest claim tokens.

# Stellar Threads: Founder Narrative & Hackathon Submission Story

## The Spark
In early 2026, while touring heritage textile mills in Northern Italy, we witnessed a heartbreaking contradiction: master ateliers handcrafting bio-silk gowns for top luxury houses were on the verge of bankruptcy. Why? Traditional factoring houses charged predatory 25% rates, and department stores took 120 days to pay invoices.

## Why Stellar?
Ethereum gas fees made 500-unit garment batch tracking economically unfeasible. Solana lacked predictable storage rent semantics. **Stellar Soroban (Protocol 22)** was the exact answer:
- **Sub-cent transaction fees** allow individual garment passport minting.
- **Predictable State Rent (TTL)** enables permanent digital provenance without unbounded ledger bloat.
- **Fast 5-second ledger settlement** delivers real-time confidence to buyers and factoring investors.

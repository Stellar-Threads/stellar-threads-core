# Fashion Atelier & Luxury Brand Onboarding Guide

## Overview
How luxury ateliers register brand identities and publish verifiable digital garment passports on Stellar Threads.

## Step 1: Wallet Connection
Connect with **Freighter** on Stellar Testnet and fund with testnet XLM via Friendbot.

## Step 2: Register Atelier
Submit brand name, textile specialties, and laboratory audit credentials to `designer_registry`.

## Step 3: Issue Passports
Batch mint digital twin passports matching physical NFC garment tags.

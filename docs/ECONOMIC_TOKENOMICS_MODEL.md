# Economic & Financial Governance Model

## Fee Distribution Architecture (10,000 BPS Engine)
For every completed B2B fashion trade escrow:
- **9,000 BPS (90.0%)**: Designer Atelier primary payment.
- **750 BPS (7.5%)**: Quality inspection laboratory attestation fee.
- **150 BPS (1.5%)**: Protocol reserve and insurance pool.
- **100 BPS (1.0%)**: Secondary garment provenance royalty.

## Invoice Factoring Return Model
- Annual Percentage Yield (APY): 6.00% to 15.00% depending on atelier tier.
- Seniority: Capital providers hold senior collateral claims against physical inventory.

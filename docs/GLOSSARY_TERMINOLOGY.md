# Glossary & Stellar Soroban Terminology

- **Stroop**: The smallest atomic unit of XLM (`1 XLM = 10,000,000 Stroops`).
- **Basis Point (BPS)**: One hundredth of one percent (`10,000 BPS = 100.00%`).
- **Soroban**: The high-performance, developer-friendly smart contracts platform on Stellar.
- **TTL (Time-To-Live)**: Ledger lifetime of Soroban contract instances and storage entries before requiring rent extension.
- **Soulbound Passport**: Non-transferable or strictly authorized digital authenticity passport.
- **Factoring**: Immediate liquidity advance against verified purchase orders or accounts receivable.

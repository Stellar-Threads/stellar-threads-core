# Regulatory & Legal Compliance Framework

## 1. European Union Ecodesign & Digital Product Passport (DPP)
Stellar Threads is designed to comply with EU DPP directives mandating digital traceability for all garments sold within the EU by 2027.

## 2. B2B Commercial Law Compliance
Milestone escrow agreements satisfy UCC Article 2 and UN Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods (CISG).
